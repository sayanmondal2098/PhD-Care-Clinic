WHY THIS EXISTS

A PhD has hard problems. It should not require carrying all of them alone.

PhD Care Clinic is a focused space for CSE doctoral researchers and emerging academics to pause, make sense of a challenge, and move forward with a clearer plan.

We bring research-aware listening to the parts of doctoral life that are often reduced to generic advice: stalled progress, uncertain direction, difficult feedback, and the next career move.
